# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Zidan Select to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information.
- Follow the project's security policy when reporting vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, sustained off-topic posting, or bad-faith repetition.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release notes, and any project space. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through https://github.com/akhtar1985. Do not use a public issue for a conduct report.

Maintainers will review reports, keep them confidential where possible, and choose a proportionate response: a private warning, content removal, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who are involved in a report must not decide its outcome.
